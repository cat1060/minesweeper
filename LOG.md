**Stage 2, issue #3: flagging.** Asked: "Build #3." Added `toggleFlag` and `isOver` to `src/game.js`. I fixed an edge case found while writing tests: clicking a cell flagged before the first click used to place the mines without revealing anything, which lost the first-click guarantee. Now a flagged cell is checked before mines are placed. On the page: right-click flags with the mouse; long-press flags on touch (400 ms, cancelled if the finger moves more than 10 px, so scrolling a wide board doesn't flag); and a Flag mode toggle makes a tap flag. Android also sends a context-menu event on long-press, which is ignored for touch so a flag doesn't toggle twice. 6 new tests bring the suite to 25 of 25 passing. Checked in the browser with simulated mouse and touch events, and with real clicks at 375px wide. What broke: `navigator.vibrate` logged an error when no real tap had happened yet; it's now guarded. Not yet verified: long-press on a real iPhone and Android phone, which needs a live URL. Next: #4 win, loss, and New game.
